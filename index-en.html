About

About the Band

MOOII (無異) is a Seoul-based indie rock band that expresses life exactly as it is. Their music blends post-punk, punk and garage rock. Since forming in 2022, they have played steadily at live clubs such as Club Bbang in Hongdae, Pet Sounds in Itaewon and Studio HBC in Haebangchon, as well as a variety of distinctive curated stages like the Namwon Yocheon Beach Festival and Jonnafe, with more than 50 shows to date. After their 2025 single "Wonjeom Italtal," they released four more tracks and will release their debut album Korean Dystopia on October 28, 2026.

The name MOOII means "business as usual." Rather than pouring in special emotions or ideas, the band aims for music as a by-product of life, which makes their music the closest and most honest to life itself.
